Why Foundation

Why Choose Us?

Developing the whole child — Socially, Physically, Intellectually, Cognitively and Emotionally (S.P.I.C.E).

🏫

Heritage-Based Education

We blend Zimbabwe's rich cultural heritage with modern teaching methods to give children a grounded, meaningful education.

🗣️

English Immersion

Our English immersion programme builds strong communication skills from an early age, giving children a lifelong advantage.

✝️

Christian Environment

We nurture children in a loving, faith-based environment that builds strong moral values, integrity and character.

🤝

Ubuntu Values

We teach children the spirit of Ubuntu — compassion, community and respect for others — building well-rounded human beings.

🌍

Whole Child Development

Our S.P.I.C.E model ensures every child grows Socially, Physically, Intellectually, Cognitively and Emotionally.

🏠

Boarding Facility

A safe, caring boarding facility available from ECD A to Grade 7, ensuring children are well looked after around the clock.
